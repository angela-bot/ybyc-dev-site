Spring Regatta

Open the season with a full day of racing, camaraderie, dinner, and awards on Yaquina Bay.

About the regatta

A spring tradition on the bay.

One or more races constitute the regatta. Racing is governed by the Racing Rules of Sailing 2025–2028, including US Sailing Prescriptions, and uses the low-point scoring system.

If seven or more races are sailed, each vessel’s worst score is excluded. Refer to the official Notice of Race for eligibility, classes, safety requirements, course details, and entry instructions.
