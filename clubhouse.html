Clubhouse rental

A flexible room and covered patio with panoramic views of Yaquina Bay, the fishing fleet, and the bridge.

The space

Business meetings, celebrations, and community gatherings.

The 30-by-40-foot meeting room opens onto a large covered patio. A day or evening rental may last up to 10 hours.

The rental includes a full kitchen, twelve eight-foot tables, and 75 chairs. The room can be arranged for banquets, presentations, classes, weddings, receptions, birthdays, and reunions.
