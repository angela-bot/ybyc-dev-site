Come aboard

Become a YBYC member.

Boat ownership is not necessary. Bring your curiosity, your skills, or simply your love of the water.

2026 dues

Choose your membership.

Prices are loaded from the club’s Square catalog. First-year Individual and Household prices shown here include the required $100 initiation fee. Life and Honorary memberships are conferred under club policy rather than purchased.

Household · New member

$480.00 first year

Includes the $100 initiation fee.

For a family unit with two or more people living in one household. Every household member over age 21 may vote and hold office.

Individual · New member

$420.00 first year

Includes the $100 initiation fee.

For a person age 21 or older who is single and the only person living in their household. Individual members may vote and hold office.

Student · Military · Crew

$130.00 / year

Student or Military: available to full-time students and active-duty military members with current identification.

Crew: available for up to two years with sponsorship by a club skipper. Crew members may attend meetings and activities, but may not vote, hold elective office, use club boats, or rent the clubhouse at the member rate.

No initiation fee applies to these classes.

Life Membership

No dues

Reserved for Charter Members and individuals who meet the life-membership criteria in the club bylaws. Life Members may vote and hold office. Once approved by the Board, membership is granted for life.

Honorary Membership

No dues

Granted for exceptional achievement or service that furthers the purposes of the club. Honorary members may attend meetings and participate in activities, but may not vote or hold elective office. Membership is valid for the calendar year in which it is bestowed.

Member benefits

Plenty of ways to participate.

  • Reciprocal rights at many yacht clubs worldwide
  • Free entry for weeknight sailboat racing
  • Selected club sailboats and powerboats
  • Discounted clubhouse use and sailing classes
  • Potlucks, barbecues, programs, and social events
  • Newsletter, volunteer opportunities, and youth sailing support
