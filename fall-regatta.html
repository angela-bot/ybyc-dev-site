Fall Regatta

Two days of racing, camaraderie, and food bring the YBYC racing season to a close.

About the regatta

You are cordially invited to participate in the YBYC Fall Regatta.

This Notice of Race includes racing information and an entry form which can be mailed to YBYC along with payment. Signed entry forms can be e-mailed to the race chair. For additional information entry forms, contact YBYC race chair via email: race@yaquinabayyachtclub.org, online at www.yaquinabayyachtclub.org, or at YBYC 750 S.E. Bay Blvd, Newport, OR 97365.

Racing will take place {{ site.events.fall_regatta | raceDate: "range" }} on Yaquina Bay, Newport, Oregon. If 7 or more races are sailed a vessel's worst score will be excluded from her series score, otherwise all races will count and there will be no discards. It will be governed by the Unites States Sailing Association’s “The Racing Rules of Sailing 2025-2028” including US Sailing Prescriptions and scored using the low point scoring system. Fall Regatta is open to centerboard boats, keel boats, catamarans and One-Design fleets which establish a class.
