Our story

At home on Yaquina Bay since 1947.

The club was founded to promote aquatic sports—particularly yachting—and preserve the traditions of navigation and seamanship.

After years of temporary meeting places, members built the current harbor-view clubhouse with volunteer labor in 2004. A floating structure added in partnership with the Oregon Boating Foundation supports youth sailing. Members of every age and walk of life now share the same camaraderie and love of being on the water.

Membership

You don’t need a boat to belong.

Join for reciprocal yacht-club privileges, free weeknight racing, social events, volunteer opportunities, member boat access, clubhouse discounts, and the monthly newsletter.

  • Member racing and regatta opportunities
  • Use of selected club sail and power boats
  • Social events and an active waterfront community
  • Support for county-wide youth sailing

Clubhouse rental

A harbor-view room for your next gathering.

A 30-by-40-foot meeting room opens to a large covered patio with views of the bay, fishing fleet, and bridge. Rentals include a full kitchen, twelve eight-foot tables, and 75 chairs.
